What people are carrying

Why Solace exists

Most of us carry something we've never said out loud — not because no one would listen, but because we're afraid of how they'd look at us afterward. Solace removes that fear by removing identity from the equation entirely. No name is ever attached to what you write, and no name is ever attached to who answers.

How it works

You share something anonymously, tagged with the kind of thing it is. Anyone browsing can read it and reply — also anonymously. Nobody, including the people who built this site, can trace a post back to a person.

What Solace is not

Solace is peer support, not professional therapy, and not an emergency service. If you or someone you're talking with is in real danger, please use the resources on the "Get help" page — they're staffed by people trained for exactly that.

Ground rules

Be kind. No judgment. Never ask for or share contact details. Posts that harass or exploit others get removed. Full guidelines are shown the first time you post.

Community guidelines · Crisis resources

Before you post

Solace works because people feel safe here. A few ground rules:

  • Be kind, even when you disagree with what someone shares.
  • No judgment — people come here because elsewhere felt unsafe.
  • Never share or ask for names, emails, phone numbers, or social handles.
  • This is peer support, not professional therapy. If someone is in real danger, we'll point them to real help.
  • Posts that harass, sexualize, or exploit others will be removed.

If you need help right now

Solace isn't equipped for emergencies — but real help is available, right now, for free:

  • US: Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
  • UK & ROI: Call Samaritans at 116 123
  • Elsewhere: findahelpline.com lists a helpline for your country
  • If you're in immediate physical danger, please contact your local emergency number.

You deserve support from people trained to give it — not just from strangers on the internet.
